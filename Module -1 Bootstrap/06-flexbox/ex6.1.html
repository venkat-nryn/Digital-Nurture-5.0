Flexbox Utility Classes Reference

CategoryClassesDescription
Direction flex-row, flex-column, flex-row-reverse, flex-column-reverse Sets flex direction
Justify Content justify-content-start, justify-content-center, justify-content-end, justify-content-between, justify-content-around Horizontal alignment of flex items
Align Items align-items-start, align-items-center, align-items-end, align-items-stretch Vertical alignment of flex items
Wrap flex-wrap, flex-nowrap, flex-wrap-reverse Controls wrapping behavior
Gap gap-0 through gap-5 Spacing between flex items
